              How routes are ranked
              1. Time comes first: each route starts from the minutes until you arrive, including any wait.
              2. Extra minutes are added for what's harder with a pram. Stairs where a lift is missing or out: ~1 flight +8, 2–3 flights +15, a deep or large station +22 (with “A few are OK”: +3, +6, +10). Lifts partly out or with no status +6, step-free only with staff help +5, each change +4, each bus ride +2, a station with a single lift +2.
              3. Walking up to 10 minutes counts as normal, a little more up to your limit, and double past it.
              4. A station with high particle levels on the platform adds +4. On hot days (feels like 27°C or more), each ride on a train without cooling adds +4, and +1 where only some trains are cooled.
              5. Stair sizes come from the station type: deep RER and train stations in Paris and big hubs 4+ flights, suburban train stations about 1, metro stations 2–3.
